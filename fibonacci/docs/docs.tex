This algorithm is different from the classic Fibonacci algorithm. This new algorithm has a complexity $o(\log n)$. The idea is to calculate the following matrix: 
$$\left(\begin{array}{cc}1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right)^{n-1}$$ Once the calculation has the result from the top left of this matrix is the corresponding Fibonacci number.\\ \\

\textsc{Exemple:}\\
Let's take an example to make it a little clearer:\\
Let's find the Fibonacci number for n = 5. Note $f(n)$ the number fibonacci. Normally the formula Fibonacci classic is $F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2)$, where $F(0)=0$ and $F(1)=1$. Then $F(5)=5$.\\
Now in use the matrix, we have 
$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right)^{4} & = & \left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right)  \left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right) \left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right)^{2} \\ 
 & = &\left(\begin{array}{cc} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1\end{array}\right)   \left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right) \left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right) \\ 
 & = & \left(\begin{array}{cc} 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 1\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{array}\right) \\
 & = & \left(\begin{array}{cc} 5 & 3 \\ 3 & 2\end{array}\right).
\end{array}$$
Hence on the top left, we have f(5)=5.
